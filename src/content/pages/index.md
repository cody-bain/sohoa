**Southern Oaks is** a beautiful and friendly town home community in Lorton Virginia surrounded by park land; Newington Commons Park to our southeast and South Run Stream Valley Park to our north. Located in the southern end of Fairfax County Southern Oaks is an oasis with all of its private town homes backing to trees and common area yet it's minutes to major transportation routes, bus lines and the metro.
