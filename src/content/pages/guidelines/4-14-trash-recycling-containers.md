title: "4.14 Trash/Recycling Containers"
description: "Containers may be placed for pickup along the curbs in front of the townhome no earlier than the evening prior to the scheduled pickup day and must be removed that same day..."
---

Containers may be placed for pickup along the curbs in front of the townhome no earlier than the evening prior to the scheduled pickup day and must be removed that same day after refuse collection is completed. Trash placed for pickup shall be placed in containers manufactured for trash storage purposes or heavy duty plastic bags and sealed. Trash/recycling containers not stored inside garages or townhomes shall be stored either under the front porch or in the rear yard.

Recycling of newspapers, cans and bottles is encouraged. However, the recycling containers are the property of the collection contractor and must remain with the townhome for the next occupant.
