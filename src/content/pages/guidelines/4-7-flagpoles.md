title: "4.7 Flagpoles"
description: "Permanent free-standing flagpoles are prohibited. However, homeowners wishing to have temporary flagpoles or staffs which do not exceed six feet in length and are attached at..."
---

Permanent free-standing flagpoles are prohibited. However, homeowners wishing to have temporary flagpoles or staffs which do not exceed six feet in length and are attached at an incline to the front wall or pillar of the townhouse may do so and need not submit an application.
