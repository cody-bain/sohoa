There are two options for you to apply to make architectural changes:

Send your request to 8328 Linden Oaks Court, Lorton, VA 22079; please be sure to include:

> 1. Your name

> 2. Address

> 3. Contact phone number and

> 4. Your [Architectural Change Application](/media/form.pdf).

You can also [email the request](mailto:ARB@sohoa.org?Subject=Change%20Request) to the ARB.

Be sure to date the request.
