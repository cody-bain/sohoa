---
title: "4.6 Exterior Painting"
description: "The exterior of any building shall not be painted a color different from the original color of said building without the proposed color having been approved by the ARB..."
---

The exterior of any building shall not be painted a color different from the original color of said building without the proposed color having been approved by the ARB beforehand. The original McCormick paint color codes are available from the ARB.
