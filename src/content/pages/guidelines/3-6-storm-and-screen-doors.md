Rising energy costs have encouraged homeowners to take measures to conserve energy through the installation of storm doors. Energy conserving measures, however, should and will be done without compromising the visual quality of the neighborhood. Prior to the installation of any door, an application to the ARB shall be submitted and approved.

### 3.6.1 Design Criteria

Doors should be straightforward without ornamentation such as scallops, scrolls, and imitation gate hinges, such as those on Crossbuck doors. Storm and screen doors should match the same color as the entry doors behind them or the same color as the architectural wood trim around the door.

### 3.6.2 Application Contents

All applications shall include the following information:

> a. Drawing, sketch and/or photograph of proposed door.

> b. Color indication of the storm/screen door and the existing front or side entrance way and/or architectural wood trim around said door.

> c. Estimated installation date.
