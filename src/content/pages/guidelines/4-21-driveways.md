ARB approval is not required in order to make repairs or re-seal a driveway provided that the homeowner maintains the same size and materials as the existing driveway. ARB approval is necessary before changing a driveway’s materials.

Driveways must be hard-surfaced and constructed of rolled asphalt or brushed concrete. Driveways must be maintained so as to be free of cracks or other structural damage.

> a. Driveways must be installed in accordance with current code (licensed contractor with a class A, B or C license for a residential driveway)

> b. Stamped concrete is prohibited for driveway surfaces

> c. Concrete pavers are prohibited for driveway surfaces

> d. Widening of driveways is not permitted
