Prior to installation of any hot tub/Jacuzzi, an application to the ARB shall be submitted and approved.

### 3.10.1 Design Criteria

Hot tubs/Jacuzzis will be permitted only at ground level in rear yard locations which shall be fully enclosed by a 6'0" high fence to all perimeters.

### 3.10.2 Application Contents

All applications shall include the following information:

> a. Site plan showing the relationship of the hot tub/Jacuzzi to the house, property line and adjacent neighbors.

> b. Picture and/or detailed drawing of hot tub/Jacuzzi to include dimensions and materials used.

> c. Estimated start date and estimated completion date in terms of days after start, not to exceed 90 days.
